  3. Bolted togetherA crane sets both modules on screw piles and the single joint bolts from inside. About one day.

What’s included

Steel frame that lasts

Galvanized steel frame on screw piles, rockwool insulation in walls, floor and roof, and steel or fibre-cement cladding. No concrete slab needed.

Its own power

Roof solar, a LiFePO4 battery and a hybrid inverter, sized to what your home actually uses. An induction hob and DC fridge keep demand low.

Its own water

Roof rainwater runs to a buried cistern and a tank under the bed, through a filter and UV unit. Greywater waters a reed planter outside.

Fresh, warm air

Heat-recovery fresh-air units, an efficient heat pump, double or triple glazing, and big glass you can point at the sun for free winter heat.

Questions

How accurate is the price?

The designer uses typical factory-direct prices for every material, fitting and system, plus transport and setup. It is a planning estimate. Your quote is a fixed price for your exact design.

What is not included?

Land, permits, site clearing, access for trucks and a crane, import duties and local taxes. The designer shows transport for the distance you enter.

Do I need planning permission?

It depends on where you build. Homes under about 25 m² have simpler rules in many places, but always check with your local authority before you order.

Can it really run off-grid?

Yes. The designer sizes solar, battery and water storage to your household and shows how many days you can go without sun or rain. In cloudy winters, plan a backup such as a wood stove.

Is it really wheelchair accessible?

The wheelchair version is. It keeps 80 cm paths, a 1.5 m turning circle in the living area and bathroom, a roll-in shower and a 1 m door, and the designer checks all of it as you change things. Our main render has a raised sleeping platform and a compact shower room, which are not wheelchair-friendly. Because the floor sits on screw piles, step-free entry needs a ramp or a platform lift, both offered.

Can homes be joined or stacked?

Wings bolt to the central module, and the stackable frame option is built for up to four storeys. Ask in your quote request.

Can I save or share my design?

Yes. Your design is kept in your browser, and you can download it as a file or copy a code to send to someone.
